About

I’m S. Ukobong (xukobong) — a developer and Web3 builder focused on practical tools that work even when the cloud doesn’t.

I build offline AI and software for constrained environments, where every byte, watt, and millisecond matters. Constraints aren’t a limitation; they’re the best design teacher.

I ship in public, share the process (bugs included), and connect with other builders, indie hackers, and founders who are figuring it out as they go.

Decentralized, yet 100% in control.
